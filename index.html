How I work with AI agents

AI speeds up delivery. Discipline keeps it safe.

01 · DEFINE

Requirements first

Business rules, acceptance criteria and open questions are written down before any code. Unknown rules block the build instead of being guessed.

02 · BUILD

Agents do the typing

Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity build on short-lived branches, and multiple agents review one another's work. Every change ships with tests.

03 · SHIP

A person approves

Nothing reaches production or leaves the building without a human sign-off. Releases are versioned, logged and easy to roll back.
